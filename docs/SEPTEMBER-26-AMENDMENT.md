# September 26 amendment

This amendment supersedes conflicting consent/profile requirements in V3-SPEC.md.

Every registration includes a fighter application/profile, regardless of visibility or content purchase. Replace per-channel permissions and the two final acknowledgements with one required, initially unchecked acceptance of the Terms of Participation & Content. Consolidate recording, editing, production ownership, publication and advertising in that draft document.

Public / Private remains: Private restricts public uses, including advertising, without changing production ownership. Under-18 profiles remain private pending an approved guardian procedure. Both adults must accept the Terms and be Public before shared footage is publishable. This preview does not submit personal data or execute a legal agreement.

## Video preparation

app/lib/hero-media.ts has separate desktop and mobile source slots, intentionally empty until the owner supplies footage. The hero retains its current poster. Once connected, a single matching video is mounted (mobile at 600px or below), with muted autoplay, loop, playsInline, pause/play control and poster fallback on error. Reduced-motion preference prevents video loading. Use direct approved MP4 URLs or local public assets, not a video-hosting watch page. Confirm mobile/desktop crops and replace the generated-visual caption when real footage is integrated.

## Ownership review

The business model is draft, not proof of secured production rights. Participant likeness authorization does not establish ownership of third-party copyright. Final contracting entity, scope, contributor agreements, removal procedure and participant license remain to be finalized.
